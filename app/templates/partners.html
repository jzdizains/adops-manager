How this works
Inviting an email as Admin gives it control of the whole Business Center — every ad account in it, current and future — so there's nothing to share account-by-account.
The invite is sent through a connected login that is already an Admin of that BC (you, since you created it). TikTok has no "invite yourself" for a Business Center whose login isn't connected here — for a brand-new BC the dashboard doesn't have a token for yet, send that first invite from the BC's own TikTok login, then it shows up here.
A BC holds at most 20 admins; an email that's already invited is quietly ignored by TikTok and stays pending below until it's accepted.
