What the API can and can't do herechecked against TikTok's API reference + help centre
Can: add a partner BC (/bc/partner/add/) and share ad accounts with it (Admin/Operator/Analyst); invite an email into a BC as Admin or Standard with ad accounts pre-assigned (/bc/member/invite/); assign a TikTok account to a member who has joined (/bc/asset/assign/, TT_ACCOUNT).
Can't (website only): share a Pixel or a TikTok account with a partner BC — TikTok's partner API accepts only ad accounts (and reads back ad accounts / catalogs). Assigning a pixel to a member isn't in the API either. That's why those two are a checklist with the deep link, not a button.
Rules: every call needs your user to be an Admin of the BC it acts on; a BC holds at most 4000 members / 20 admins; invites already sent are silently ignored by TikTok; a pixel can only be shared once it's owned by the BC (Pixels page → move to BC).
