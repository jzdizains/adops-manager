How this worksverified against TikTok's reporting guide
Once a day a background job asks TikTok's /report/integrated/get/ for each account: the hourly delivery of today and the last two days (basic report by stat_time_hour, one day per call), and yesterday's audience report for age × gender, country, state, OS, placement, network, language and device brand — one call per breakdown, skipped for accounts with no delivery that day. TikTok computes audience data 10–12 hours late, so the tables end yesterday and the day before is pulled twice to catch late rows. Rows are stored per campaign per day for 45 days; the filters above only read the store, so they are instant. Conversions are TikTok-attributed (the optimization event), not Glitchy postbacks.
