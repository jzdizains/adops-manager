How this worksverified against TikTok's reporting guide
Background jobs ask TikTok's /report/integrated/get/ and store the answers per campaign per day (45 days kept); the filters above only read the store, so they are instant. Hour-by-hour delivery (basic report by stat_time_hour, one day per call) is near real-time on TikTok's side, so today's hours are refreshed every {{ hours_every }} min for accounts with active campaigns. Audience breakdowns (age × gender, country, state, OS, placement, network, language, device brand — one call per breakdown, skipped for accounts with no delivery that day) are computed by TikTok 10–12 hours late, so today + yesterday are re-pulled every {{ breakdown_every }} min for active accounts to pick up rows as TikTok publishes them, and a full pull for every account runs once a day. The tables end yesterday unless “include today (partial)” is ticked. Both intervals are on the Settings page. Conversions are TikTok-attributed (the optimization event), not Glitchy postbacks.
