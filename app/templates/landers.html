How a lander goes live
  1. Create it here: texts, colours, where Continue goes (the next page or per-rule destinations), the escape method per platform (test them under Tools › Escape test first).
  2. ⬇ Package → upload index.html to the folder on your domain (Hostinger, Vercel, anywhere static). Put that URL in the preset's landing URL as usual.
  3. Change the next URL, rules or escape method here any time — the page reads them from {{ track_host or '…' }}/t/l/<slug>.json at open. Texts, colours and the pixel are baked in: re-download after changing those.
What the page never does: cloak, show fake states, fire pixel events without a real tap, or trap the back button. In a real browser it simply continues.
