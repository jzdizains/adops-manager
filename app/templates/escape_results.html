How to run it2 minutes by hand · a $5 ad for real numbers
By hand (right now): send the URL to yourself in a TikTok DM, open it from inside TikTok on an iPhone and an Android, tap Continue. The result shows on the phone and here.
On real traffic: launch a tiny campaign with this URL as the landing page (any preset; the source param does no harm). Every visitor who taps Continue becomes a row here, with the TikTok version they run.
What the button does: Android → Chrome intent; iPhone → x-safari- scheme; if the page is still visible ~1 s later it continues in-app. The final page reads the browser's identity — that's the proof.
Escape lab — every technique, one button each the real answer for your phone and today's TikTok build
Android: Chrome intent, package-less intent (default browser), VIEW+BROWSABLE intent, intent via popup, Chrome's scheme, and a Play-Store handoff check.
iPhone: x-safari (navigation and popup), the Shortcuts x-callback trick, Chrome / Firefox schemes, a Safari-search handoff check and an App-Store handoff check.
A method that lands as escaped is usable. left means another app opened but no page landed (the handoff checks are expected to do this). stayed means TikTok swallowed it.
