{# ---- Connect a creator: 3-step pop-up ---- #}
How do you want to connect the creator?
Authorise a spark code · the code is checked with TikTok and saved to your Sparks list
Link the creator's TikTok profile · done in TikTok, approved by the creator
1
Open the Business Center the ad accounts belong to
Business Center → Assets → TikTok accounts → Add TikTok account → Request access, and enter the creator's handle.
{% for b in bc_list %}{{ b.name or b.bc_id }} ↗{% endfor %}
2
The creator approves it in the TikTok app
They get a notification (Settings and privacy → Business Center requests). Until they tap Approve nothing changes — chase them, not the dashboard.
3
Assign the profile to the ad accounts
Back in Business Center → Assets → TikTok accounts → the creator → Assign → tick the ad accounts (or Ads Manager → Assets → Identity on each account).
4
Refresh here
Press Refresh on this page (or Next) — the creator shows up as a Business Center identity on every assigned account, and their authorised posts can be launched or Auto-grabbed.
No API can do this on the creator's behalf — TikTok requires the profile owner's consent, which is why this step lives in TikTok.
