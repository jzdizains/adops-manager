How this worksverified against TikTok's API reference and help centre
1. Each slow sweep (and “Scan now”) reads every account's ads. An ad whose status is AUDIT_DENY (the ad or its ad group failed review) lands here with TikTok's own reason and suggestion from /ad/review_info/.
2. With auto-appeal ON (Settings → Appeals) an appeal goes out through /adgroup/appeal/ with your reason text — unless the reason matches one of your skip keywords{% if keywords %} (currently: {{ keywords|join(', ') }}){% endif %} or today's cap is used up. Those wait here for you instead.
3. TikTok allows one appeal per rejection, and once an ad group (or any ad in it) was appealed, no second appeal is offered — so the engine never files twice, and “Appeal all” is deliberate. A rejection appealed in Ads Manager by hand is picked up too and simply tracked.
4. The answer arrives as APPEAL_SUCCESSFUL / APPEAL_FAILED, then APPEAL_DONE once the ad group was re-reviewed; wins and losses also show in the Inbox.
